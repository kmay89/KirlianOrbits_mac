KIRLIAN ORBITS
A circular music machine — notes orbit, a glowing arm plays them.

✨ Never touched music?

Just press start — it plays a song instantly. Pick a Starter Song up top (try the 🎵 Music Box tunes), then click inside the ring to add glowing notes. They snap to the key, so it always sounds good.

🎹 Musician?

It's a polar sequencer: radius ≈ pitch (quantized to the chosen key/scale), angle = phase. A Bézier arm sweeps and triggers notes on crossing. Give each note its own orbit speed (BPM-% or clock divisor) for polymetric patterns. Built-in synth — no MIDI.
